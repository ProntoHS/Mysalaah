The Qur'an text, transliteration and translations in this folder come from quran-json
version 3.1.2 (https://github.com/risan/quran-json), which carries the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence.

    Arabic text:      the Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia, https://quranenc.com
    Transliteration:  Tanzil, https://tanzil.net/trans/en.transliteration

That licence requires attribution, which is why this file exists and why the app names
the source on screen. It also requires that anything derived from this material is shared
under the same licence.

Verified on conversion: 114 surahs, 6236 verses, every surah matching the canonical verse
count, in Arabic and in all five translations, with no verse left empty. That checks the
shape of the text, not the words themselves.
