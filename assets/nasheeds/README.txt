Nasheeds that ship with the mat go here, and nothing is here.

A nasheed is a recording somebody made and owns. The artists worth listening to are on
labels, and the sites offering "copyright free" collections of them are aggregators who do
not hold the rights to give away -- so the mat ships as a player rather than a library.

The owner's own recordings go in ~/.salaah/nasheeds on the mat. That folder is never
shipped, never updated and never looked at by anybody but the family who filled it.

If something is ever licensed to ship here, it needs a LICENCE.txt beside it saying what the
track is, who it is by, and on what terms -- and nasheeds.json giving each file a title and
an artist. The test suite refuses audio in this folder until that LICENCE.txt exists, because
"we will sort the licence out later" is how an mp3 ends up in a release.
